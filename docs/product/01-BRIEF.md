# 01 — Brief: YouTube Content OS

## Product definition
- **Client/mode:** Grupo Monoga, internal, production.
- **Primary operator and final decision-maker:** Daniel Monoga, Spanish-speaking technical builder/creator.
- **Current problem:** useful signals from reference channels and evidence from Daniel's real work do not have a repeatable low-effort path into an original, privacy-safe recording brief. Ad hoc synthesis competes with the work that should create the channel's authority.
- **Current process:** real work → informal reference discovery → scattered ideas → manual synthesis → delayed/skipped recording.
- **Primary action:** review a bounded digest from 5–10 allowlisted channels, add sanitized evidence from real work, and approve one weekly mission brief/script.
- **Administrative value:** one private queue makes sources, evidence, cadence, privacy, and approval traceable.
- **Business outcome:** consistent authoritative publishing without stopping the underlying work or copying competitors, optimized for recurring viewers per extra production hour.

## Position and rules
Daniel documents how he turns real problems into verified systems using software, AI, and mechatronics: decisions, failures, and evidence, not hype. “Si las cosas de cabeza no te funcionan, voltéate para verlas desde otro punto de vista” is a worldview, not repetitive copy.

- Pillars: Jarvis 30%, MARAL OS 25%, Universidad OS 20%, mechatronics 25%.
- Four weeks: 3 edited long-form videos + 1 replacing monthly live; 2 derivative Shorts/week; 1 community post/week.
- Maximum extra production time: 3 hours per long-form video.
- Human approval is mandatory before publication; internal data must be sanitized/demo-only.
- Do not download/scrape third-party videos/transcripts or collect commenter identities.
- Channel IDs remain blank/configurable until manually verified; never guess.
